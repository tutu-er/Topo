                                                                                                           VIII. C ONCLUSION
                                                                                    This paper proposed novel topology estimation and fault
                                                                                 detection algorithms. To this end, four new algorithms were
                                                                                 devised. The first estimates the voltage sensitivity coefficients
                                                                                 of distribution networks more accurately by considering vari-
                                                                                 ability in the transformer voltage. This is shown to increase the
                                                                                 accuracy of the sensitivity coefficients. The second algorithm
                                                                                 uses these sensitivity coefficients to estimate the topology of
                                                                                 the network. It improves on previous algorithms by placing
Fig. 9. Moving mean of σ̂n /σn as a function of time for fault case 1. Phase     additional constraints on the solution reflecting the physical

the fault detection day provided by the DNSP. The fault development few
days before the detection date can be clearly seen.                              are always connected by service lines. This new algorithm
                                                                                 reduces the suboptimality according to the DeltaCon metric.
                                                                                 The third algorithm is a new tree similarity metric that is
                                                                                 shown to capture global structure better than metrics such as
                                                                                 DeltaCon. The global changes it notices, such as swapping
                                                                                 meters between feeders, is more important than small scale
                                                                                 structure, such as swapping the order of meters along a feeder.
                                                                                 Finally, a new fault detection algorithm is proposed that allows
                                                                                 for online fault detection, and has been shown to estimate
                                                                                 faults earlier than other methods. The sensitivity estimation
                                                                                 works for arbitrary networks with meters at every load and
                                                                                 generator, however further work would be required to extend
                                                                                 the recursive grouping and similarity estimation algorithms
                                                                                 to meshed networks. The simulation case studies confirm the
                                                                                 superior performance of the proposed algorithms compared to
