# Two fixed saved H: pairwise-cone AC scale judgment

The histories are exactly the first two distinct saved pilot identifiers, H930001 and H930002. Within each H, the filename-first spatial record and anchor `history_star_0` are used. Both saved forecasts were independently verified as exact means of their H-only anchor P/Q, same-junction source and root states. No truth, realized future noise, selector or formal calibration/evaluation was used.

Both H completed all 108 tasks and both fixed max200 starts: 216 tasks, 432 fits, 3130 total nfev, maximum 16 nfev. All 216 task keys and 1728 serialized physical states independently replayed. H1 used 1918 actual AC calls and 106.031398 seconds; H2 used 1721 calls and 99.268137 seconds. No numerical/physical failures or budget extensions occurred. Both H have raw task manifests, compact108 tables, full ratio/Taylor/step summaries and 25-file exact executed-source snapshots. The first-H source version and tasks remain recoverable after the explicit H-id parameter was added. All 19 frozen science sources and original 119 records remain unchanged.

At epsilon=0.25, all 36 tasks per H match the original finite-box local value within the predeclared [0.85,1.15] diagnostic band. The actual AC/finite-local ranges are [0.99849875,1.00413249] for H1 and [0.99831260,1.00568512] for H2. The finite-witness Taylor residual norms divided by epsilon squared are saved at every scale; maxima are about 0.10347 and 0.08979. The observed scaled shared/state steps remain finite. These are finite point checks, not uniform nonlinear remainder or global near-optimality guarantees.

H1 has a common terminal-1 X lower-bound activity, so its free nuisance projection is a relaxation; the original one-sided tangent and finite box are separately recorded. H2's common center is interior, and its free and original-tangent costs coincide numerically. For each H, the cone's positive primal/dual certificates cover only the two projected binary-rational columns after numerical SVD. Full-matrix and finite-box KKT/minorant values remain numerical; the unconditional nonlinear cost lower bound is zero.

The new positive competitor direction has little effect in H1: 18/72 other-cherry task entries have a reduction above 1e-8, the smallest cone/star ratio is 0.99989179, and no distances are zero. H2 has 12/72 reductions; its smallest ratio is 0.89508153. The meaningful H2 reductions occur in four fixed pair/direction/action combinations, repeated at three scales: clade12 -> clade01 for the R direction under both actions, and clade01 -> clade12 for the X direction under both actions. They are confirmed by the original-domain AC comparisons: for example, constant0 clade12 -> clade01 R at epsilon=1 has star cost 1.33824051 and other-cherry cost 1.19862270 (ratio 0.89567061). These tasks show that the omitted positive compensation can matter at a legal fixed point; they do not establish a new control benefit.

Read-only aggregation under the existing H-only task weights gives a much smaller overall correction. For the declared two positive directions at epsilon=1, the weighted H+N half-distance decreases by 0.00735%/0.00614% for H1 constant/alternating and by 0.16475%/0.04743% for H2 constant/alternating. The two fixed-action orderings remain the same. This arithmetic uses H+N task levels, not a zero-EV increment or a newly selected action, and cannot be reported as a comparison of deployed policies.

The evidence supports retaining the mathematical correction and diagnostic module. It does not yet justify implementing a new selector: the observed fixed-task correction is concentrated in low-distance directions and is small after the frozen H-only weighting; practical discrimination, star/weak risk and extra computation were not improved or evaluated here. Work stops after these two H as requested. No H replacement, extra directions, extra controls, higher fit budget or formal run was used.
