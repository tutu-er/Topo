## What the extra-error objective does and does not fix

Whole-family minimum separation is identically zero when both historical
parameter sets contain the same zero-extra-block explanation. Comparing final
errors relative to the best feasible menu under each hypothetical model avoids
that particular identity. It does not make the risk easy to compute, remove
weak-block ambiguity, or establish a WZZT-specific advantage.

Three counterexamples remain:

- All menus may miss an arbitrarily weak block, yielding zero extra error but
  high absolute error. Do not interpret low extra error as recovery.
- Zero-effect models can have different false-block risks across menus under
  noise; their risk/extra error is not automatically zero.
- A fixed-support LP's minimum objective does not fix its coefficient vector,
  prediction on other inputs, or contraction. Freeze the actual code, solver
  settings, call order and handling of unresolved output before defining risk.

Use effective positive support to merge a declaration that only adds zero
coefficients. Do not merge different positive structures that merely agree on
the observed history. The comparator menu is chosen outside the response
expectation, never after seeing participation.
